# inventory management

Controls stock thresholds, stock messages, counts, and inventory-status colors.

## Where to find it

Open **Customize → Theme settings → Inventory management**.

## Settings

### General

| Setting                      | What it controls                      |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Low inventory threshold      | Adjusts low inventory threshold.      |
| Very low inventory threshold | Adjusts very low inventory threshold. |

### Visibility

| Setting                           | What it controls                                                                    |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Show inventory notice             | Chooses show inventory notice. Options include: Low/very low inventory, Always.     |
| Hide backordered inventory notice | Controls whether backordered inventory notice is hidden.                            |
| Show product count                | Chooses show product count. Options include: Never, Low/very low inventory, Always. |

### Style

| Setting        | What it controls     |
| -------------- | -------------------- |
| In stock       | Sets in stock.       |
| Low stock      | Sets low stock.      |
| Very low stock | Sets very low stock. |
| No stock       | Sets no stock.       |
| Backordered    | Sets backordered.    |
